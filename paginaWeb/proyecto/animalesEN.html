    Meet Our Animals

    In our zoo, you will find an amazing variety of animals, from mammals to reptiles, each with fascinating characteristics that make them unique in their species.

    • Lion: The king of the jungle, known for its golden mane and mighty roar.
    • Elephant: A giant of the savannah, famous for its intelligence and large ears.
    • Tiger: The largest feline, known for its strength and characteristic stripes.
    • Eagle: A majestic bird of prey with incredible vision and exceptional hunting skills.
    • Flamingo: A long-legged bird known for its pink color and long neck.
    • Peacock: Famous for its spectacular tail with bright and beautiful feathers.
    • Crocodile: A water predator, known for its large size and powerful jaws.
    • Snake: A legless reptile, famous for its ability to slide and its hunting skills.
    • Turtle: An animal with a hard shell that moves slowly but lives a long life.
